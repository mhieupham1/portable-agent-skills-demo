# Product request

Let workspace members keep a shared todo list. They should be able to add a
task, assign it to a member, and mark it complete. Everyone in the workspace
should see the same list.

No decisions have been made about who may edit or delete another person's task,
whether due dates need time zones or recurring tasks, how completed tasks are
retained or reopened, how members are notified, or how simultaneous edits are
resolved.
